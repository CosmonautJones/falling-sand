How this vessel works

Drag to build a straight line, or tap for a stamp. Stone holds its shape; sand falls when released. You can build only in empty space inside the dotted region. The reservoir, walls and receiver are protected. An unaffordable line places nothing. Undo returns its full cost.

Release opens the valve and locks construction. The cistern must stay above its quota for 60 simulation ticks by tick 900. Pause and hidden tabs stop flowing time; Step advances one tick. Retry restores this exact vessel and inventory. Your sandbox saves and discoveries stay in the sandbox.

Keyboard: focus the vessel, use arrows to move the cursor, Shift for ten cells, and Space to place a stamp. Standard buttons control release, pause, step and retry.
