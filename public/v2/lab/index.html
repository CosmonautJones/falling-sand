Experiments leave a trace. Painting and placement are practice; a reaction writes the page.

Make a beautiful mess.

Pick a material and drag inside the vessel. Use Heat, Cool or Blast to start reactions. Reverse gravity to turn a familiar scene inside out.

The Matter Codex remembers what your experiments create. Unwritten pages hold clues; research milestones earn new supplies. Choose All materials workshop in the library for unrestricted building. Painting and placing materials do not earn pages, but reactions do. Clearing the vessel keeps your journal.

  • Blueprints are real simulated materials. Place a building, reactor or creature habitat, then experiment.
  • Capture freezes the world while you select a rectangle. Save and reuse your invention, or export it as a JSON file.
  • Save world stores the current simulation in this browser. Export a world to keep a portable copy.
  • Gravity moves grains and disconnected structures. Structures joined to a vessel edge stay anchored. Creatures still use their own movement rules.
  • Heat and Velocity lenses reveal what the particles are doing. The heat scale is qualitative; exotic materials use playful rules.

Keyboard: Space pauses; P/E/H/C/B/X select tools; R rotates; M mirrors; Z undoes; Esc cancels. Focus the vessel, move with arrows and press Enter to act. Hold Shift with arrows for larger steps.
