# Catch the Pour — bounded prototype

Catch the Pour is an optional authored objective using V2 physics. It opens separately from free play, with independent storage and a dedicated worker. The sandbox, flasks, discoveries and experiments keep their current semantics.

Three concepts were considered. Catch the Pour makes cause and success spatial: route a finite water charge with finite construction. One Spark has immediate drama, but scheduled/ambient ignition and collateral combustion complicate a fair single-spark rule. Gold Contract uses existing transmutation, but reusable catalysts, fonts and visitors complicate fair input/output accounting. Neither alternative is implemented. Council reports distinguish actual sandbox play from these analytical comparisons.

The first scene has exactly 2,000 water cells, 160 stone and 240 sand cells. Build only into empty cells inside the marked region; terrain, source and receiver are protected. Preview and commit use the same unique cell footprint. Overlap costs zero. A stroke that exceeds remaining inventory is rejected in full. Undo refunds accepted construction before release. Release locks construction.

Catch at least 1,320 water cells in the outlined receiver for 60 consecutive committed simulation ticks, before tick 900. Occupancy is the goal; it does not claim that the fluid is motionless. A drop below quota resets the streak. Failure shows current catch and permits immediate retry. Retry creates a fresh world with the same fixture, RNG, inventory and counters, and cancels old browser work through a fresh worker. Hidden pages pause the challenge rather than accruing wall time.

A diagnostic fixture probe at tick 900 (without stopping on success) found 1,206 cells retained with no construction, 1,407 with a small cap near the left opening, and 1,937 with an upstream divider. Contract tests must prove baseline failure and two distinct winning builds, complete replay and fair accounting. These are authored solutions, not proof of general puzzle solvability or engine conservation for all materials.

The prototype has one scene. Separate material cost and committed completion ticks support replay without collapsing them into one arbitrary score. Records use only a challenge-specific storage key; storage failure cannot prevent play. No new materials, creatures, engine rewrite, continuous rewind, staging infrastructure or deployment is included.

Existing V2 creatures already use probabilistic local rules, local sensing, cargo and a shared diffusing trail field. Persistent individual hunger/energy, inherited traits and obstacle-aware scent are proposed follow-ons, not implemented intelligence. See the council evidence for the source-based distinction.
