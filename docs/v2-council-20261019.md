| Catch the Pour | A visible route, finite construction and readable near-misses | Fixed reservoir, accepted-cell budgets, protected receiver, tick goal, exact retry | Selected by the user; one authored scene implemented |

No council member played the two unimplemented alternatives. The two Catch solutions are different construction topologies: a small cap near one drain and an upstream divider. They establish a choice between cheap and fast results, not broad campaign depth.

Primary design research was supplied by the parent and checked against the publishers' own pages. [Opus Magnum](https://www.zachtronics.com/opus-magnum/) explicitly supports open-ended simplest/fastest/compact solutions; separate cost and time records are our application of that idea. [Sandtrix](https://msky-dev.itch.io/sandtrix) uses a simple spatial color-connection predicate; a visible receiver is our inference for V2. [Noita](https://noitagame.com/) demonstrates a material-simulation game context; it does not prove this puzzle is fun. These games were researched, not personally played in this council.

## Creature intelligence: existing versus proposed

V2 already uses CA-style probabilistic local rules. Mites sense nearby cells, prioritize danger, score food/kin/hazard/trail moves and retain cargo in shade state. Minnows use local schooling/food/hazard scores. A diffusing, decaying shared trail field provides environmental memory, but crosses walls and is not obstacle-aware navigation.

Individual accumulated hunger/energy/age, inherited traits, learning and path planning are absent. Some ecology is source-consuming, but the engine does not enforce whole-world mass or energy conservation. The October 6 self-sustaining ecosystem discussion is design direction, not proof of those systems or an explicit choice of creature-intelligence architecture.

A separate bounded follow-on could give one species tiny persistent needs/state, explicit danger → forage → rest/wander priorities, then add a coarse obstacle-aware scent field only if local sensing is insufficient. Fixed update order, state and RNG must be saved. A seed alone does not guarantee replay. This is a proposal; no creature behavior changes were made here.
